Verification Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to all technician registrations.

TechConnect maintains the integrity of its platform through a strict, automated certificate verification process. Every technician profile must pass this verification before becoming visible to clients. This policy explains how the system works and the consequences of submitting fraudulent documents.

3.1 Automated Validation System

When a technician submits their registration, our automated system performs the following checks in real time:

1

Document Type Check

The system reads the uploaded document using OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and verifies that it matches the selected qualification type (Certificate or Degree).

2

Field of Study Cross-Check

The system scans the document text for keywords that match the declared Field of Study and Skill Category. A mismatch between the document content and the registration data causes instant rejection.

3

Certificate Number Recording

The certificate reference number is recorded in our database. This number is used for manual cross-verification with the Rwanda TVET Board when required, and to detect duplicate or reused certificates.

4

Field-Skill Consistency Check

The system independently verifies that the entered Field of Study and the selected Skill Category are logically consistent with each other — preventing mismatched trade claims.

3.2 Real-Time Feedback on Failure

If any verification check fails, the registration is blocked immediately and the technician receives a specific error message explaining exactly what went wrong:

Error: "The uploaded document doesn't look like a Certificate. Please check your Qualification Type."

Error: "We couldn't find [your field] mentioned on the uploaded document. Please upload the correct certificate."

Error: "[Field of Study] doesn't seem related to the Skill Category you selected. Please make sure both describe the same trade."

Failed uploads are automatically deleted from our servers. The technician may correct their submission and reapply with the accurate documents.

3.3 Fraud Prevention & Permanent Blacklisting

⚠️ Zero Tolerance for Document Fraud

Submitting a digitally altered, forged, expired, or otherwise fraudulent certificate or diploma is a serious violation of our platform's integrity. When fraud is detected or reported:

  • The offending certificate number is permanently blacklisted across the entire platform — it can never be used to register again.
  • The associated account is immediately and permanently banned with no possibility of appeal.
  • Any active subscription fee is forfeited in full — no refund will be issued.
  • The incident may be escalated to the Rwanda TVET Board and relevant law enforcement authorities.

This policy exists to protect the thousands of honest, hard-working graduates who rely on this platform's credibility to find employment.
