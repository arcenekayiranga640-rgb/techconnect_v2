All subscription fees are strictly non-refundable. Because technicians retain 100% of job earnings generated through the platform, subscription fees compensate for listing visibility only — not job outcomes. Refunds will not be issued for unused days, low client traffic, or any other reason.

The 200 RWF client contact access fee is non-refundable once contact information has been unlocked. This is analogous to purchasing telecom airtime — the service is consumed at the moment of access and cannot be reversed.

All users are required to act honestly and in good faith. The following actions result in immediate and permanent account termination:
