Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Compliant with Rwandan Data Protection Law.

TechConnect is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and how you can have it removed. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third party for commercial purposes.

2.1 Data We Collect

We collect the following data strictly for platform functionality and identity verification:

👤

Identity Data

Full name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address — provided during registration.

🎓

Qualification Data

Certificate/diploma files, certificate reference numbers, field of study, and skill category — used exclusively for verification.

📍

Location Data

Province, district, and sector — used to match technicians with nearby clients.

💳

Transaction Data

Phone number and payment confirmation code submitted when unlocking contacts — retained for fraud prevention.

2.2 Certificate & Document Security

🔒 Your documents are protected

  • All uploaded diploma and certificate files are stored in protected server directories that are not publicly accessible via any URL.
  • Certificate files are accessible only by the platform administrator for the sole purpose of identity verification.
  • Your documents are never made public, never downloadable by clients or other technicians, and never sold to any third party under any circumstances.
  • Files are encrypted at rest and transmitted only over secure HTTPS connections.

2.3 What Is Publicly Visible

Your profile has two visibility tiers:

🌐 Visible to Everyone

  • Full name
  • Trade / skill category
  • Province, district, sector
  • Short bio / experience
  • Qualification type & field
  • Verification status

🔒 Hidden Behind 200 RWF Paywall

  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Email address

Accessible only after a client pays the 200 RWF access fee.

2.4 Account Deletion & Data Removal

You have the right to request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion:

  • Your profile is immediately removed from public search results.
  • All uploaded certificate and photo files are permanently erased from our servers.
  • Your personal data (name, phone, email) is purged from our database.
  • This action is irreversible and complies with Rwandan Data Protection and Privacy Law (Law No. 058/2021).

To request deletion, log in and use the "Delete Profile" button on your profile page, or contact us at support@techconnect.rw.
