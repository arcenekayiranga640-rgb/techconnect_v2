Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to all visitors of TechConnect.

TechConnect uses a minimal, privacy-first approach to cookies. We use only the cookies that are strictly necessary to operate the platform. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics that monitor your behaviour across other websites.

4.1 How We Use Cookies

We use essential session cookies only. These are temporary cookies stored in your browser during your visit and automatically deleted when your browser session ends (unless otherwise noted below). They are required for the platform to function correctly and cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

Cookie Name

Duration

Purpose

session

Browser session

Keeps technicians securely logged in while they navigate the platform. Expires when the browser is closed.

client_access_expiry

24 hours

Tracks the active 200 RWF contact access pass. Grants the client access to all technician contacts for exactly 24 hours from payment confirmation.

lang

Browser session

Remembers your preferred language (English or French) so you do not have to reselect it on every page.

is_admin

Browser session

Grants secure access to the admin dashboard after successful authentication. Cleared immediately on logout.

4.2 What We Do NOT Use

🔒 No tracking. No advertising. No third parties.

  • No advertising cookies — We do not serve ads and do not allow ad networks to place cookies on your device.
  • No third-party analytics — We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar tracking tools that monitor your behaviour across other websites.
  • No cross-site tracking — Our cookies are strictly first-party and are limited to this platform only.
  • No persistent fingerprinting — We do not use device fingerprinting or any other technique to identify you beyond the session.

4.3 Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. However, please note:

⚠️ Disabling cookies will break core features

Because we only use essential cookies, disabling them will prevent you from staying logged in as a technician and will remove your 24-hour client access pass. The platform may not function correctly without session cookies enabled.

To manage cookies in your browser, visit the settings/preferences page of your browser. Instructions are available for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
